# Characters

## Character Creation Guidelines

- Randomly generated characters are encouraged! Rather than carefully crafting a character from the start, we will let the dice decide what our characters' starting points are, and learn who they are at the table.
  - Picking and choosing from the tables is fine if you would like to do that.
  - If you would like something that is not on the tables in the book, please just talk to the GM and I will do my best to say yes to any reasonable ask.
  - This [mouse generator](https://mausritter.com/mouse) can help you quickly generator a character
- For the early part of the campaign, please have your character be a mouse resident of [Littlebreeze](./thawhallow/littlebreeze.md). As we discover new settlements and species, I am open to being flexible with more place of origin and expanding to other mouse sized creatures.
- Please always try to have a backup mouse created and ready to go to accelerate getting back into the game if your character dies.
